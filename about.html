About Robin Hoffpauir

Revenue Leader for API and Enterprise Platforms • GTM, Partnerships, Forecasting, Team Execution

I build predictable revenue engines for usage-driven platforms. That means turning technical products into clear commercial motions, tightening pipeline discipline, improving forecast accuracy, and scaling teams that win consistently in competitive enterprise cycles.

Today I operate in the geospatial and cloud ecosystem, leading go-to-market alignment, co-sell strategy, and expansion plays across complex stakeholder maps.

Overview

I am a sales leader with 8+ years across enterprise SaaS, cloud, and data platforms. My lane is owning growth by building repeatable motions: clear ICP and positioning, partner leverage, multi-threaded enterprise sales cycles, and operational cadence that keeps deals real and forecasts honest.

I currently serve as Solutions Sales Manager at Navagis, a Premier Google Maps Platform partner, where I lead go-to-market alignment with Google Cloud teams across North America. The work lives at the intersection of platform adoption and expansion, partner co-selling, and the operational reality of usage-based economics.

Before Navagis, I scaled channel and CSP growth at ISI Analytics, delivering 132% quota attainment across eight consecutive quarters. Earlier, I founded and exited Parlayking LTD, a SaaS analytics startup built on Bayesian models for predictive compensation analysis. That experience made me fluent in translating messy data into decisions a team can execute.

Approach & Philosophy

My operating model is simple: align, diagnose, implement, execute. Alignment means shared definitions (ICP, stages, exit criteria, and what "qualified" actually means). Diagnosis means looking at data and deal reality, not vibes. Implementation is building the system (cadence, plays, enablement, partner motion). Execution is coaching and inspection, with the same standards every week.

I love the art of relationships, but I respect the math. The goal is a team that can explain why revenue will happen, not just hope it does. When the process is tight, creativity becomes a multiplier instead of a fire drill.

Leadership Style

I am a player-coach who runs toward clarity and accountability. I coach to behaviors (discovery quality, multi-threading, next steps, mutual plans), not just outcomes. I build a culture where the scoreboard matters, and so does how we get there.

I operate cross-functionally by default. Strong sales leadership is equal parts customer insight, product feedback loop, partner coordination, and RevOps discipline. If any one of those breaks, the forecast becomes fantasy.

Why I Fit a Head of Sales Role (Mapbox-style)

  • Usage-based revenue fluency: I think in adoption, expansion, and retention, then map that to pipeline and forecast rigor.
  • Partner leverage: I build co-sell motions that are operational, measurable, and repeatable, not just "relationships".
  • Enterprise execution: I run multi-threaded cycles with mutual plans, crisp next steps, and tight qualification.
  • Systems builder: I install cadence, stage definitions, enablement, and inspection that makes performance predictable.
